# HS-180-07 — The arc retrospective

- **Project:** holdspeak
- **Phase:** 180
- **Status:** backlog
- **Depends on:** HS-180-01
- **Unblocks:** HS-180-09
- **Owner:** unassigned

## Problem

The arc from 170 to 180 is the longest planned sequence in HoldSpeak's
history. What worked, what hurt, what carries forward must be named
before the arc closes, so the next arc (if there is one) starts from
honest ground.

## Scope

- In:
  - The retrospective covers:
    - The method: one phase at a time, design before build, the
      owner's word per canvas, the walk per phase, the census, the
      Constitution. What helped? What slowed the work? What would
      change?
    - The tooling: the Delivery Workbench, the UX canon scanner, the
      UAT framework, the live walk harness. What was useful? What was
      ceremony? What is missing?
    - The team: the Fedaykin model, the orchestrator, counsel. What
      was the ratio of build time to proof time? Where did delegation
      break?
    - The product: which faces did the arc touch? Which were rebuilt?
      Which were only swept? What is the coverage of the Tuesday
      question across faces?
  - The retrospective is filed in the phase folder.
- Out:
  - Chartering the next arc (that is the owner's call after 180).
  - Organizational decisions.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The retrospective covers method, tooling, team, and product.
- [ ] Honest: what worked is named; what hurt is named; what carries
      forward is named (Article VI.1 -- the product states its own
      limits).
- [ ] Filed in the phase folder.

## Test plan

## Notes / open questions

- The retrospective is for the owner. Its value is in honesty, not
  length. A one-page document that names three things per category
  is more useful than a ten-page report.
