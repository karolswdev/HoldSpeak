# Phase 148 — The Menu Grammar: the exit record

Written 2026-08-29 at the close. Chartered and delivered in one
sitting, 6/6, from the owner's words ("a little too sterile… a
tribute to the incredible Amiga OS… the top menu toolbar… really
poor, right?") to menus that answer them.

## What the owner sees now

Open Go: a launcher — thirteen programs each wearing the same glyph
they wear in the dock, the deck, and ⌘K; the four daily apps split
from the nine tools by a recessed separator; every shortcut in a
drawn keycap well. Open Object with nothing selected: unmistakable
stipple ghosts that erase to the panel's own ground (the Commodore
way), one quiet "Select an object" footer instead of eight echoes,
the minority reason inline, F2 and Delete still visible in their
wells. Right-click a window head: the registry's own labels and
keycaps, acting on THAT window. Press ArrowDown after opening a
menu: it walks — on real Chromium, not just in a test. And the
whole glyph question stays the owner's: A/B/C are one attribute
apart, forever.
