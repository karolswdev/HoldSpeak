## The felt walk (yours; ~15 minutes)

Start from the project you actually work — name it out loud first.

1. **Open it from the desk.** Search or double-click; the memory
   window should open with its Timeline face. Does the timeline read
   like YOUR project's history?
2. **Find a decision by text.** The Search face; type something you
   remember deciding. Open the hit.
3. **Follow it to its moment.** From the Decisions face, jump to the
   transcript segment. Is the moment right?
4. **Change your mind for real.** Accept something true; supersede
   something outdated (pick its successor in-row). The superseded
   row must wear its successor.
5. **Promote something real.** A decision that deserves an ADR — the
   verb, no ceremony. Then "Draft with model" on your `.43` profile
   and read the draft with fresh eyes.
6. **Ask the project a question you know the answer to.** Read the
   citations before the answer. "Grounded on N of M" — open one chip.
7. **Watch the machine work.** Open the Processes window; dictate
   something; watch the operation appear and end.
8. **Delete-and-survive (optional, on a scratch meeting).** Delete a
   meeting; its decisions must survive marked `source deleted`.
