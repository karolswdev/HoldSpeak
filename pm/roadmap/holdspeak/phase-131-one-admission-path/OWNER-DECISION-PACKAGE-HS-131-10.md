## Owner ruling — charter all five amendments

**Ruling accepted on 2026-08-12:** charter all five amendment stories. This is
the smallest atomic wave that does not hide a finding:

1. **Residual service admission** — migrate Cadence, the second Decisions
   route, and dormant Delivery review under authenticated domain parents and
   frozen revisions; then delete `build_intel_for_target` and its transitional
   marker.
2. **Plugin provider admission** — migrate the fourteen builtin providers plus
   `segment_probe` onto the host-injected admitted engine or dispatch handle;
   then delete or privatize uncontextual provider construction.
3. **Speech side-door admission** — give dictation dry-run and the CLI command
   authenticated session parents and frozen plans, or make the chosen paths
   lexical-only with no provider construction.
4. **Mesh receiver authority** — cryptographically verify the admitted
   envelope and warrant, or admit and receipt the physical work through a
   node-side `InferenceRunner`.
5. **Meeting residual admission** — delete or admit dormant MIR; replace the
   config-time live engine with the frozen admitted plan plus a liveness flag;
   route bookmark labeling through `_admitted_bookmark_label`.

The chartered design beats choose delete versus admit for Speech, Mesh, and
Meeting. Until all five amendments land, HS-131-10 remains blocked and the docs
and walk stories remain held. The owner's ruling amends the phase charter; it
does not amend Article XI under Article X.

## Reservations for the sitting

1. The source proof claims literal model verbs. A non-literal computed name
   such as `getattr(intel, dynamic_name)` is intentionally outside the AST
   claim; dynamic model execution remains prohibited by review and runtime
   context enforcement.
2. If cancellation wins after the first provider-dialect failure but before
   follow-up admission, the logical invocation outcome is cancelled while the
   only physical child's immutable receipt remains failed. Both facts are
   exposed; neither receipt is rewritten.
3. A pre-dispatch refusal or post-registration cancellation may have one
   admitted child and terminal receipt with zero physical leaf work. The
   universal equality is `child_operations == terminal_child_receipts`;
   strict physical equality applies to dispatched children.
