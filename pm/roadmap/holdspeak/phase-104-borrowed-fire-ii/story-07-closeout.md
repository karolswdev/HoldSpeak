## The bar

Article IX: proof over claim, and the phase closes on the owner's
sitting, not the agent's assertion. The walk below is the machine
proof; the sitting is the human one. Both are recorded here before
any box in the charter flips.

## The walk (live, real metal, one continuous session)

Staged via a composed UAT recipe (extend the HS-103-05
steering-demo recipe rather than minting a parallel one — one
recipe family for the agent-steering surface):

1. **Arm the gate** on a real Claude Code session (real process,
   real repo, the HS-104-02 rig): flip both opt-ins, confirm the
   ledger and doctor read the armed state.
2. **Hold and approve:** induce a matched Bash call; the card rises
   on the shade; Approve; the command runs; the audit row reads
   back.
3. **Hold and deny with reason:** induce another; Deny with a
   one-liner; the reason appears verbatim in the agent's transcript;
   the agent visibly course-corrects.
4. **The crown cases, live:** expiry denies; hub restart mid-hold
   invalidates (the two-process kill, Phase-89 style); the unarmed
   fast path adds no latency (timed).
5. **PR receipts:** a registered source shows a real PR with state,
   CI rollup, observed-at, and its attribution label; See-diff
   renders the real local diff; the stale path shown by yanking the
   network once.
6. **Session receipts:** the same session's pull-out line shows all
   three tiers behaving per HS-104-05 (including the removed-price
   absent-cost check).
7. **Sweep:** full pytest (metal exclusion per standing rule), web
   suite + build + tokens gate, all guards, census checks — outputs
   written to files and READ before any flip.

## The sitting

The owner drives the same seven beats hands-on (workstation glass
only — per the standing direction, no iPad leg exists or gates
here). Verdict recorded verbatim in this
phase's status file. A rejected beat reopens its story; the closeout
does not absorb fix work (the Phase-101 loop discipline).

## Chef's notes

- Schedule the sitting after the machine sweep is green, never
  interleaved — a sitting on a shifting tree wastes the owner's
  verdict (the Phase-103 second-sitting lesson).
- The restart beat is the one most tempting to fake with a mock.
  Don't: kill the real process. The difference has caught shipping
  bugs in this repo three phases running.
