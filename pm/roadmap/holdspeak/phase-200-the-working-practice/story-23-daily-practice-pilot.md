## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Sample five relevant meetings, ten recall tasks, two updates, and five Interview sessions including three revisits.

- [ ] At day ten, report each target as pass, fail, or inconclusive and preserve the original denominators.
- [ ] Advance configurable daily practice only with a recorded owner usefulness verdict and no unresolved critical factual defect.
