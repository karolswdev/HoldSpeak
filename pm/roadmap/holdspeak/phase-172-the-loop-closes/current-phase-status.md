## Goal

Meetings produce decisions, commitments, and 1:1 prep instead of a
recording. Intelligence runs by default after every meeting linked to a
Room, on the local model (170's assignment). Extracted decisions and
action items arrive as PROPOSALS confirmed through the kernel (Article
IV: voice arms, it does not fire). The People ledger gains a resolver
between display names / aliases and Watch entity assignees / reviewers,
staying inside the encrypted boundary. The 1:1 brief reads from
Watches, commitments, and meetings. The tagline becomes true: "Record a
meeting, it closes the loop."
