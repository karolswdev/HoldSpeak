# HS-116-08 — The morning brief

- **Project:** holdspeak
- **Phase:** 116
- **Status:** done
- **Depends on:** HS-116-05, HS-116-07
- **Unblocks:** HS-116-09
- **Owner:** unassigned

## The thesis (the bar)

The first shipped autonomous workbench. A Morning Brief workbench
that collects overnight signals, synthesizes a short briefing, and
delivers it as a desk notification the owner sees when they open the
Desk. This proves the entire stack end-to-end: constitutional
context → recipe → skills → scheduled conductor → fresh session →
item processing → receipt → notification. When this ships, the owner
wakes up to a personalized morning brief generated by their own
agent, on their own infrastructure, with honest egress.

**Articles served:** III (egress — the brief runs where the owner
put it and says so), V (consent — the brief is a proposal, not an
action; it reads and summarizes, it never sends or files), VI
(honest by construction — no demo state; the brief is real or empty,
never faked), IX (proof over claim — the brief ships with a receipt
showing what ran, where, and how many tokens).

## Deliverables

1. **Morning Brief template.** Added to the template registry
   (HS-116-05). Recipe system prompt:

   > You are the Morning Brief agent. Your job is to prepare a
   > concise morning briefing for the owner. For each item in your
   > workbench, read its grounding (meetings, artifacts, notes) and
   > produce a structured summary. Your output format:
   >
   > **[Item title]** — [one-line summary]. [Action needed: yes/no].
   >
   > End with a "Today's focus" section: the 1-3 most important
   > things from all items, ranked by urgency.
   >
   > Never fabricate. If an item has no grounding, say "no context
   > attached." Never propose actions beyond the owner's explicit
   > items.

   Schedule suggestion: `0 7 * * 1-5` (7 AM weekdays).

2. **Collector items.** The morning brief workbench's starter items
   are collection tasks:
   - "Yesterday's meetings" — grounded to the meetings primitive
     (last 24h of meeting transcripts).
   - "Open workbench receipts" — grounded to other workbenches'
     overnight run receipts.
   - "Delivery status" — grounded to the DW state feed (if DW is
     wired): current phase, next story, blockers.

3. **Synthesis item.** A final item (lowest priority, runs last):
   "Write the morning brief." Its grounding includes the results of
   all collector items from the same run (Hermes `context_from`
   pattern). The agent sees: constitutional context + collector
   outputs + "write the brief."

4. **Delivery.** The synthesis result is delivered as:
   - A desk notification (RuntimeBus SSE → notification in the
     dock).
   - A durable artifact on the Desk (auto-kept, like the "Keep"
     verb in PersonaChat).
   - The workbench's run receipt.

5. **Wake gate in practice.** If no meetings occurred, no
   workbenches ran overnight, and no DW updates exist, the
   collectors return empty results and the synthesis item skips
   with a one-line receipt: "Nothing to brief." No tokens wasted
   on an empty day.

6. **End-to-end proof.** This story is not done until the morning
   brief runs on the real hub with a real inference target, produces
   a real brief from real data (even if seeded for the proof), and
   the receipt shows honest egress.

## Test plan

- `uv run pytest -q` — morning brief template instantiation,
  collector items populate, synthesis item receives collector
  outputs, wake gate skips on empty data.
- Integration: set up the morning brief workbench on the real hub,
  seed a meeting transcript and a DW state update, trigger manually,
  verify the brief appears as a desk notification and artifact with
