## Problem

Zone and knowledge membership reach MCP through direct service calls (`holdspeak/mcp/tools.py:864-879`) and HTTP through the routers (`holdspeak/web/routes/primitives/directories.py:111-133`, `kbs.py:99-119`). Decision delete, status and supersede bypass the contract on HTTP (`primitives/decisions.py:98,108,125` call `_svc()` directly) and on MCP (`desk.delete kind=decisions` through `_primitive_delete`, `holdspeak/mcp/tools.py:614-615`; `decision.supersede` through `primitives.supersede_decision`, `:1043-1044`). Decision status has an HTTP path but no separately named MCP tool. No desk write admits through the kernel: `holdspeak/services/primitive_service.py` has no kernel call, and Phase 5 left `decision.create/update` as INHERITED Article XI DEBT, pinned by a characterization test (`tests/unit/test_philo5_the_loop.py:618`; `../phase-5-the-one-service-layer/current-phase-status.md:329,332-335`). Filing is one zone per primitive: a re-file moves it (`holdspeak/db/schema.py:1605-1611`, `holdspeak/db/primitives.py:1224-1235`). Supersede writes two rows (`primitive_service.py:220-232`).

An AGENT principal (a remote MCP credential) writes desk primitives today with zero kernel operations (Astra r2 finding 4 reproduced it). The kernel refuses a non-owner decision without a live owner parent (`holdspeak/kernel/broker.py:190-204`); a live parent qualifies only when its signed continuation identities name the agent (`holdspeak/kernel/causation.py:46-68`). Palette membership is not approval. So an agent's admitted write needs a real grant that the kernel recognises. The owner ruled it (R1): bounded delegation, granted once on the desk; no HELD state in Phase 7.

A malformed attempt at a consequential operation leaves no receipt today: `invalid_arguments` and `authority_in_arguments` escape before the service (`holdspeak/operations.py:645,649,658`). The owner ruled it (R2): a refusal receipt. Some refusals happen before the registry `invoke` is ever called, so a registry-only fix misses them (Astra r3 finding 3, reproduced through an isolated real hub: zero registry calls, zero operations, zero receipts): the duplicate `decision_id` refusal (`holdspeak/operations.py:670`), the MCP schema refusal (`holdspeak/mcp/tools.py:767`), non-object MCP arguments (`holdspeak/mcp/server.py:402`), a non-object HTTP body on decision creation (`holdspeak/web/routes/primitives/decisions.py:50`), and the palette refusal (`holdspeak/mcp/tools.py:1176`).

## Preconditions

- **The lifecycle design beat** (`docs/internal/ORCHESTRATION.md:143`; Astra r3 findings 2 and 4). One short design, checked by the other brain BEFORE this story's implementation brief. It settles, and names a fence for each: (1) the grant id and terms hash frozen at admission, approval never substituting a newer row; (2) expiry inside the hashed terms, as `holdspeak/services/schedule_delegation.py:37`; (3) the execution cutoff at the executor seam (`holdspeak/kernel/executor.py:61`) and the revoke/expire/re-grant interleavings between admission, approval and execution; (4) a refusal during approval terminalises the operation with a refusal receipt (`holdspeak/kernel/broker.py:204` raises without one today); (5) the credential rule: a reissue keeps the principal identity and revokes the old credential (`holdspeak/principals.py:149`), credentials are gone after a restart (`holdspeak/principals.py:103`); the grant says whether it survives a reissue and a restart, and issue, expiry, revoke, reissue and the row's chip agree. The charter states these invariants only; the beat decides the answers.
- **The canvas** for the grant's verb and chip, ratified by the owner before the face is built (Scope, "The grant's place on the desk").

## Scope

- **In:**
  - Descriptors for `zone.file`, `zone.unfile`, `zone.members`, `kb.member.add`, `kb.member.remove`, `kb.members`, `decision.delete`, `decision.status`, `decision.supersede`; HTTP, MCP tools and the `holdspeak://zones/{id}/members` resource over `invoke`; the bypasses retired; identities 1, 6, 25, 28-33 paid (9 MCP).
  - Kernel admission and one terminal receipt per logical operation for EVERY row the phase status's admission table marks ADMITTED (the D3 set, the four readings the owner confirmed in R4, and the hidden filing effects). Story 01 moved these rows onto the contract without changing how they execute; they gain their admission HERE.
  - The kernel path without a HELD state (phase status, "The complete kernel path"): submission; approval inline for an OWNER principal (the owner's gesture, `docs/internal/CONSTITUTION.md:184-187`) or by the kernel under a LIVE desk delegation for an AGENT principal; execution; terminal receipt. An AGENT principal without a LIVE delegation is REFUSED at admission with a refusal receipt, never held.
  - **The delegation grant** (R1): `delegation.grant` and `delegation.revoke`, owner-only operations, each itself ADMITTED (it changes authority, XI.1) with its own receipt. A grant names ONE agent identity (the credential's principal identity) and the enumerated operation set (R1's list as the phase status's "The delegation grant" enumerates it; `decision.delete` IS in it by the owner's R5 at ratification, 2026-09-25). It is a LIVE row in a new kernel table beside `kernel_schedule_delegations`, with its terms hash, the delegator (the owner), an optional expiry and REVOKED/EXPIRED states: the same shape as the schedule-delegation precedent (`holdspeak/services/schedule_delegation.py:42-70`; `holdspeak/kernel/schedule_delegated.py:17-31`). The kernel reads it at admission (no LIVE grant → refused with a receipt) and recognises it at approval beside its existing authorities (`holdspeak/kernel/broker.py:190-204`) and stamps the receipt with `delegator_kind`, `delegator_identity` and `authority_basis = desk-delegation:<id>:<terms_sha256>` (the receipt join already carries these fields, `holdspeak/kernel/journal.py:21-24`).
  - **The grant's place on the desk** (a face change, amended from r2's "no face change"): the existing approval face does not fit. `web/src/desk/gate.ts:44-58` lists HELD per-call gate proposals (`/api/gate/proposals?state=held`) with approve/deny; Phase 7 has no held state and a standing grant is not a per-call decision. The grant belongs to the agent's credential, and the credential already has its face: the Remote Access credential ledger in Settings (`web/src/pages/cores/SettingsCore.tsx:238`, rows from `:627`, the issue verb at `:719`). So the grant is ONE library `Button` verb per credential row and one chip. The words name only what the grant controls, the delegated filing and decision actions; plain edits stay exempt (R4), so "Stop desk writes" would be false (Astra r3 finding 5). Proposed strings (ASD-STE100; the canvas's to ratify): verb "Allow filing" / "Stop filing"; chip "FILING ALLOWED" / "FILING STOPPED"; no chip before any grant. A small canvas, designed on the library, shows the grant, revoke and failure transitions at 1440 and 393, and is ratified by the owner BEFORE it is built (UX canon: design on the canvas before build). No new permissions screen.
  - **Refusal receipts** for every class the phase status names (Refusal receipts), including R2's contract refusals of an identifiable consequential attempt and class 4, the adapter refusals before `invoke` (the rule: authenticated + identifiable operation + consequential → a kernel refusal receipt).
  - **The receipt readback:** the write's result carries `operation_id` and the terminal receipt; `GET /api/kernel/read?refs=operation:<id>&view=receipt` (`holdspeak/web/routes/system/kernel_routes.py:24-33`) and ONE new MCP read operation over the same `kernel.read` (`holdspeak/kernel/broker.py:37-62`) return the same receipt. The read operation is read-only (no admission, XI.5), declares its palette exposure, and is counted separately from the residual retirement (it raises the public tool count by one).
  - The principal from the transport, unchanged; the characterization test replaced by a real fence.
- **Out:** admission for the exempt rows of the admission table (D3; R4); owner-only authority for desk writes (a policy change; not applied); a HELD state or any continuation for a held write (R1); a delegation for any operation outside the enumerated set; the atlas cases (story 03); any face change other than the delegation grant's verb and chip.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every membership and decision operation above is one explicit descriptor, reached by HTTP, MCP and (where it exists) the resource, resolving to the same live instance in one hub; the HTTP bypasses at `primitives/decisions.py:98,108,125` call `invoke`.
- [ ] Every ADMITTED row of the admission table makes exactly ONE kernel operation and ONE terminal receipt per logical operation, through HTTP and MCP alike (refusal and failure included); supersede's two rows are one operation (the successor id minted before submission); a KB update that renames and changes members is one operation. Red first: on a `git archive` copy of main the same real-producer call makes zero operations and zero receipts. The hidden effects are fenced by their EFFECT: a zone delete that unfiles, a `member_ids` write that adds and removes, a `kb.create` over an existing `kb_id` that clears memberships, a zone move by `parent_id` or by `zone.create` over an existing id, a Thought tombstone that unfiles.
- [ ] OWNER path: an OWNER-principal write is submitted, approved inline by the same principal (no second confirmation, `CONSTITUTION.md:184-187`), executed once and ends in a terminal receipt, all inside the one call.
- [ ] AGENT path WITH a grant: an AGENT-principal write in the granted set is approved by the kernel under the LIVE delegation, executes at once, and its receipt names the delegation (`authority_basis = desk-delegation:<id>:<terms_sha256>`, `delegator_kind = owner`). Fenced through the real remote route with a DESK credential issued through the real settings route (Phase 5's boundary-fence form, `../phase-5-the-one-service-layer/current-phase-status.md:219`).
- [ ] AGENT path WITHOUT a grant: the same write is REFUSED at admission with a refusal receipt naming the rule (`desk_delegation_required`); the membership or decision is unchanged; nothing is held (zero operations in `awaiting_decision`). The same for a REVOKED grant (`desk_delegation_revoked`), an EXPIRED grant (`desk_delegation_expired`), a grant to ANOTHER agent identity, and an operation outside the granted set. Fenced through the real remote route. Red first: on main the same agent call writes with zero receipts.
- [ ] The grant: `delegation.grant` and `delegation.revoke` by the OWNER principal each make one kernel operation and one terminal receipt; by an AGENT principal each is REFUSED with a refusal receipt (`owner_principal_required`); an agent can never grant itself. A grant is not read from the palette: a credential with the DESK palette and no grant is refused (the fence above). No write is approved by a principal other than the owner or a LIVE grant (no manufactured owner authority).
- [ ] The face: the credential row shows the grant verb and the chip exactly as the ratified canvas; every verb the library `Button`; the RENDERED row fenced after each change (grant, revoke, a refused grant or revoke) at 1440 and 393; `readable_text` (visible, in viewport, unobscured); no verb or chip reads "desk writes".
- [ ] The lifecycle: every invariant and interleaving the checked design beat names has its fence through the real kernel (revoke, expire and re-grant between admission, approval and execution; a reissued credential; a restart); a refusal during approval ends in a terminal refusal receipt and zero operations stay in `awaiting_decision`; the chip agrees with the kernel in every case.
- [ ] Refusal receipts (V.2, XI.2; R2), one fence per class, each through BOTH transports where the class is reachable on both: (1) kernel refusal at admission (the delegation refusals above, `declared_capability_required`); (2) domain refusal after approval (the Thought filing guard `services/refinement_thought_service.py:1369-1372`, `NotFound`, `zone_name_taken`); (3) contract refusal of an identifiable consequential attempt (`invalid_arguments`, `authority_in_arguments`; `holdspeak/operations.py:645,649,658`), which keeps its named error, the transport principal and zero effects AND leaves a refusal receipt naming the rule; (4) the adapter refusals before `invoke`, one fence per path through the real adapter: a duplicate `decision_id` on a decision update over HTTP and MCP (`holdspeak/operations.py:670`), MCP `zone.file` without `directory_id` (`holdspeak/mcp/tools.py:767`), MCP `zone.file` with non-object arguments (`holdspeak/mcp/server.py:402`), a non-object HTTP body on decision creation (`holdspeak/web/routes/primitives/decisions.py:50`), and a palette refusal of a tool that names an ADMITTED operation (`holdspeak/mcp/tools.py:1176`); each keeps its named error and zero effects AND leaves a refusal receipt; each is RED ON MAIN, where the same call leaves zero receipts. Each fence asserts the unchanged state AND the receipt. The protocol-refusal boundary is fenced too: `unknown_operation` (`operations.py:606`), an unknown tool name, a failed read, a palette refusal of a read or exempt tool, a non-object payload for a conditionally admitted operation, and a malformed attempt at an exempt operation leave NO receipt (no effect is invented to journal it).
- [ ] Readback: the write's result carries `operation_id` and the terminal receipt; `GET /api/kernel/read?refs=operation:<id>&view=receipt` and the MCP read operation return the same receipt; an agent's read of another agent's operation is REFUSED (`principal_read_scope_required`, `holdspeak/kernel/broker.py:51-52`), fenced; the read operation leaves zero kernel operations; its palette exposure is declared and tested through dispatch.
- [ ] No double admission: a write through `desk.verb`, `desk.update` or the HTTP route makes one operation, not two.
- [ ] The exempt rows are NOT admitted: a note create or edit, a plain note delete, a Thought save, a zone or KB rename (after story 01's repair), `zone.create` without `directory_id`, `kb.create` without `member_ids` and `kb_id`, and `kb.delete` leave zero kernel operations (a fence, so an accidental admission is caught).
- [ ] `tests/unit/test_philo5_the_loop.py:618` (characterization) is replaced by the admission fence; its docstring history is kept in the story notes.
- [ ] Existing authority preserved except the named change: compat tables per operation (three-state) include the principal cases. The ONE named change (R1): an AGENT write that main performs without a receipt is, on the branch, refused without a grant and executed with a delegation receipt with one. Stated in full in the compat tables. No other refusal where main accepted.
- [ ] The residual fence: identities 1, 6, 25, 28-33 paid; a new identity fails (red on a copy of main); the roster and `operations.json` regenerated and drift-guarded; the new read operation reported as +1 public tool, separately.
- [ ] Discovery words for "put a decision on my review list" and "file a note into a zone" point at the admitted tools; the story 01 catalogue-only fence stays green.
- [ ] Fence law: every behavioural fence is red pre-fix through the real producers (real services, the real hub, the real lock and config paths — no test double that lies about the field the check reads); new structural invariants are proved by deliberate mutations that turn the fence red. An import failure or an unavailable symbol is not the required red.
