## Laws learned

- **A transport success is not delivery.** Keep the native proof and say what it proves. ACCEPTED BY RESEND is not an inbox observation. A malformed or inconsistent rejection body is UNKNOWN, not permission to retry (`evidence-story-07.md:209`).
- **Freeze the bytes that will leave.** Parse the preview from those bytes and admit their digest. A second serialization at Send creates a second contract. Exact read-back means exact bytes; trimming a newline can hide a broken promise (`assets/story-03-proof/captures.md:12`; `evidence-story-06.md:19`).
- **Once dispatch starts, recovery does not dispatch.** The committed boundary separates a known refusal from a possible external effect. File read-back or UNKNOWN closes the interrupted attempt; a replay does not make another post (`design/send-lifecycle.md`; `evidence-story-01.md:18`).
- **The receipt must survive where the click leaves the face.** Close, reopen, Back, failed reads and late responses are part of the action. Fence the rendered transition. Use one result source and a durable order, not a timestamp assumed unique (`assets/story-02-proof/captures.md:58`; `assets/story-04-proof/captures.md:9`).
- **A test double must speak the producer's language.** An invented `wpc-` key cannot prove the `wpc_` connection join. Mint through the real check and replace only the external process or HTTPS edge (`assets/story-04-proof/captures.md:74`).
- **A census must reject what it cannot read.** Skipping a formatted code made the face-word fence green while unknown words escaped. Also state the boundary: a hand-pinned tool mapping is not a derived call graph (`evidence-story-05.md:32`; #694 r5).
- **An owner-authenticated model call is not the owner's press.** Judge the effect and the arguments. Preserve ordinary work; do not repair one authority escape by removing the job (`holdspeak/mcp/tool_authority.py:1`; #694 r1–r5).
- **Prove wrappers fail.** A successful tail, grep or copier cannot turn a failed build or suite green. Exercise the failure combinations before trusting the capture (`evidence-story-05.md:32`).
- **Run the actual atlas and retain each run separately.** Count case × width × run, not a sample or a batch label. Name blocked prerequisites and inherited failures. Resend needed real atlas cases, not just a component test (`evidence-story-05.md:188`; `evidence-story-07.md:215`).
- **Keep proof scoped to the authorized target and state.** A scratch HOME does not isolate a native keychain. Recording-edge rehearsals use a memory key store. A reshoot from a DB copy must prevent sends. Never rerun the real-target job merely to improve a screenshot (`evidence-story-06.md:19`, `:42`; `evidence-story-07.md:217`).
- **Do not clean shared scratch space broadly.** Story 01 records deleting all `tmp.*` directories under the shared temporary root during ENOSPC recovery; sibling HOMEs may have been included. Own the cleanup path. The separate standing rule also holds: no blanket restore loop, and never touch untracked shots (`evidence-story-01.md:68`; `docs/internal/ORCHESTRATION.md:212`).
- **Check new scope before briefing in every fresh context.** Read TWO-BRAINS.md at the start, then have the other brain check the new scope before the brief. The #701 pre-build omission remains a historical fact; the owner waived the retroactive exercise. A dispatched Astra lane never supplies Muad'Dib's check by invoking Claude itself (`docs/internal/TWO-BRAINS.md:52`, `:194`; `checks/final-summary-muaddib.md`; #701 merge record).
