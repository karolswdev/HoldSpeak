# Phase 2 - The Graph

**Last updated:** 2026-09-22.

## Goal

Audit the whole platform as one graph, edges to interfaces to connections to actions, so that every verb provably closes its chain and every state the backend can produce has a face. Both brains run both passes, static and live, from one brief; a council reconciles them; what must be built is codified as Philo Phase 3, the engineering phase. The Tuesday result: the owner opens a receipt and something opens; he generates a brief and sees it; and the desk is measured against its roots.

## The roots (the owner, 2026-09-22)

> "Don't forget our roots here, because the 'desk' has taken now so much over that we forgot we're actually intended to work 90% of our time on the desk, with beautiful, cohesive interfaces that guide us, and all of this grounded in the ideology of Workbench 2.0+ on steroids."

Every pass, every finding and the council's verdict is read through that lens and the Seven Tenets. This is not a plumbing audit; the plumbing is audited because a dead verb is a face that stopped guiding.

## Authority (the owner, 2026-09-22)

- The audit is Philo Phase 2. What must be built is Philo Phase 3, chartered by the council and ratified by the owner.
- Both brains do BOTH passes (static and live), independently, from the one brief. Neither owns a side. Then a council (TWO-BRAINS §3: one round each; dissents recorded verbatim; the owner rules dissents).
- Doc drift found by the sweep is ledgered, then corrected in a lane (story 07). Product defects are ledgered, then built in Phase 3. Nothing is fixed mid-sweep. (The two sitting defects of 2026-09-22 were fixed under HS-202-06 before this ruling, on the demo-day exception.)
- The audit VERIFIES and EXTENDS the Phase 1 package (`docs/generated/*`, the Philo skills, the doc-claims registry); it does not create a parallel document pile. "Didn't we just go through a whole exercise where we created a shit-ton of documents…? Base this prompt on updating them."
