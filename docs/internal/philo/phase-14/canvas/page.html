+ New species (proposed; the library lacks them)

  • DeskIcon: an object on the glass. The mold sprite over its name; selected = the sprite's _sel image and the name inverted.
  • IconLamp and the count notch: one 12 px bevelled lamp on an icon (asks, works, done, held); the count on the ember plate, never zero.
  • IconGrid and IconShelf (B): a drawer's contents as icons; one labelled row per kind.
  • ObjectList: the drawer's list view. Bevelled sort headers, flat fact columns, a row is one press target, one lamp per row. No [ ].
  • DrawerHead: a drawer's own head (the Room's facts in one line) with the Icons / List view strip.
  • GetInfo: an object's identity and facts (a window in A and C, a column in B's tray).
  • ConfirmLine: the YOLO hand in one line: item, agent, mode, branch; Brief one press away; Cancel; Hand.
  • DragGhost and DropTarget: the lifted sprite and the lit target.
  • AskWell: the agent's question, the answer field with its mic, Enter sends, the draft one press away.
  • TimelineRail (A, B) and TimelineTrack (C): a lane's events on a rail or as stations.
  • PRCard and FilesChanged: the PR inline (checks, review, branch) and the files with their line counts.
  • NeedsRow: a Needs-you row is the object: sprite, name, one fact, one lamp, its own verbs.
  • B only: Shelf, Pit and AgentBay. C only: Zone (a place with its sign) and Trail.
  • Sprites the mold lacks: action item, pull request, person (one per person), smart drawer, the Conductor drawer, a repository apart from a project drawer.
