# Recon 2026-10-13: why the desk feels like a gimmick

Owner, 2026-10-13: "It still feels more of a gimmick than a fucking OS."

One walker used main @ 616a9a172 as the first user. His own database (a
sqlite backup), his own config, his LAN engine, an isolated hub, nothing
sent off the machine. 1440x900 and 393x852 (his phone). 84 screenshots are
in the session scratch dir (`scratchpad/recon/shots/`); each finding names
its shot number. Nothing here repeats a fixed U01-U37 item. This is what is
there now.

His real desk, copied: 0 meetings, 0 projects, 0 people, 1 note of his
("I'm just a simple note"), 8 seed notes. Five days after the fresh start,
the product holds one sentence of his. That is the baseline.

## 1. The verdict

It feels like a gimmick because the desk is a stage set, not a workshop.
The windows, icons, menus and dock look like an OS, but the objects on the
desk cannot be acted on (7 of 11 Object verbs are disabled on a note), the
two AI verbs he would use every day (Ask AI, Draft update) end in silence,
the same fact is told in four places with four numbers, and every window
speaks in chips (`PROPOSAL · LINKED · UNREVIEWED · DUE · UNKNOWN`,
`READS · NOTHING`, `KEPT · IN INBOX`, `CTX 0.0K/16.4K`) instead of words.
A real OS is boring: one way to open, one way to act, one place for each
thing, and the verbs always work. This desk has eight atmospheres
("Last Laundromat"), a wiring diagram for engines, a node graph, an
Expose, two desks' worth of dock buttons, and no reliable way to turn a
thought into a task. The chrome is finished; the work is not.

## 2. Root causes, ranked by how much they hurt his week

### R1. The verbs that matter do nothing, and say nothing

Evidence:
- Ask AI, the one question an architect asks ("What do I owe Mira this
  week?"): 4 presses, 122 s of waiting, then `NOT ANSWERED · THE HUB
  REFUSED IT · ASK KEPT · EDIT THE ASK`. No reason. The same engine had
  summarised a meeting 14 s earlier (shot 047).
- Room ▸ Draft update: 1 press, 91 s, nothing. The face still says
  "No updates yet. Draft the first one." (shot 061). The hub has no draft.
- Dock ▸ Terminals: 2 presses, no window (shot 057).
- Thought ▸ "Choose an engine": opens Runs on, which already shows
  "Answers and thoughts · Qwen3.8 27B · Ready". There is nothing to choose
  (shot 016). The thought still says `NO ENGINE YET`.
- Object menu on his own note: Ask AI "unavailable", Continue in thread
  "not threadable", Edit "not editable", Rename "not renameable",
  Duplicate "cannot", Delete "cannot" (shot 083).

OS principle broken: a verb on the screen is a promise. UX-CANON A.11 says
the same: a verb that does nothing is a lie.

Change (one day each):
- Ask AI: print the hub's refusal reason on the face, in words, and make
  the default ask a question, not "Summarize the following in 3-4 tight
  sentences" (shot 039). Then fix the refusal; a desk-wide ask with no
  grounding must still answer from the desk.
- Draft update: show the run (engine chip, elapsed, the error) under the
  button, exactly like the meeting summary does. A press that does nothing
  for 90 s is the worst press on the desk.
- Object menu: a note is editable, renameable, duplicable, deletable and
  askable, or the row is not there. Withhold the row; never grey it with
  an excuse.
- Terminals: remove from the dock until it opens something.

Cut: the "Choose an engine" button on a thought when an engine is assigned.

### R2. One fact, four tellers, four numbers

Evidence (one afternoon, one meeting):
- "What needs me": bell `5`, dock "Missed" (a dark drawer, a foreign
  material, shot 013) with `NEEDS ATTENTION · 8` (four identical
  "Dictation needs review"), Needs-you window "Nothing needs you", a
  "Needs you" desk icon with its own `5` badge, Brief `5 THINGS WAITING`.
- A commitment from the meeting ("Write the rollback runbook", owner Mira)
  shows in: meeting REVIEW (`UNREVIEWED`), meeting OUTCOMES (`TO CONFIRM`),
  Needs you (`TO CONFIRM`), Today ▸ TO DECIDE, Today ▸ Follow-through,
  The week. It does NOT show in Mira's 1:1 prep, the one place he would
  look for it (shot 036). I had to type it in by hand.
- The meeting has two review loops. REVIEW: Confirm ×6, "Accept
  reviewed", then "Accept 4?". OUTCOMES: the same five rows, still
  `TO CONFIRM`, Confirm ×5 again (shots 032, 035).
- Two Briefs: the Brief chair window and Today ▸ BRIEF.

OS principle broken: one object, one place. A file is in one folder.

Change:
- One attention source: the Needs-you window is the list; the bell is its
  count; the dock "Missed" drawer is cut; the desk icon badge is cut.
- One review of a meeting: the OUTCOMES face owns Confirm/Dismiss; the
  REVIEW wing shows the same rows read-only (or is cut).
- A confirmed commitment with an owner who is a Person appears in that
  person's prep as "they owe you" with no further press. This is the one
  link that would make People worth opening.
- Cut the Brief chair window; Today ▸ Brief is the brief.

### R3. The desk talks in chips and phase words

Evidence, all on faces he saw in the first hour:
- Thought: `ONE QUESTION · NO ENGINE YET`, `READS · NOTHING`, `KEPT · IN
  INBOX`, `EVERYDAY CONTEXT · 2 NOTES` (shot 018).
- Meeting: `PROPOSAL · ○ LINKED · ○ UNREVIEWED · ⚠ DUE · UNKNOWN · OWNER ·
  TOMAS` on one row (shot 030); `MTG 10-13 · 13:53`.
- Ask AI: `SESSION · NO TRAFFIC`, `GROUND THIS ASK`, `CTX 0.0K/16.4K ·
  USES ASSIGNMENT`, `Uses group · 192.168.1.43:8080`.
- Speak: `HOLD NONE ANYWHERE`, `LANDS IN · FOCUSED APP · DRY RUN`,
  `LISTENER FAILED` (shot 052).
- Brief: "Question did not complete / not set up: Set up local AI
  downloads this model · Mon 23:55 / Ack / Defer" (shot 001).
- Search: a MEMORY hit prints raw JSON `{"thread_id": "th_3934…",
  "fact": {…}}` and a `MEANING` chip (shot 020).
- Settings foot: `NOT SET · WRITTEN 13:47`. Delivery: `✕ NO LIVE SOURCE ·
  SOURCES · WORK · READ 14:05:07`.
- `THIS DEVICE` appears three times in one thought window, four in the
  brief.

OS principle broken: ASD-STE100 (Tenet 4) and UX-CANON A.3: tokens, verbs,
counts, names. These are not names; they are state machine labels.

Change: one pass per face, word by word, with the rule "would he say it
out loud?" A row carries at most two chips: when, and who. State goes in
the verb (`Confirm` means unconfirmed). Delete every egress chip that
says THIS DEVICE; show a chip only where something leaves the machine
(UX-CANON A.9). Search never prints JSON.

### R4. Windows pile up and come back

Evidence:
- Every door opens a new window at (24,72) over the icons; after Jobs 1-5
  the hub held 10 windows (shot 043). The meeting opened exactly 24 px
  offset over the Meetings list, hiding it (shot 026). A toast "MEETING
  READY" rendered inside three different windows at once.
- Settings ▸ "Choose engines" opens Runs on as a SECOND window and retitles
  Settings to "Settings · Runs on". Closing Runs on by its gadget, by ⌃W
  and by the hub API: it came back to the front three times (shots 042,
  043, 047). MORE SETTINGS stayed buried under it.
- A thought window is 820x640 for one sentence; Needs you is 700x650 for
  "Nothing needs you" (shot 013); the Room is 800x600 with "Nothing open".
- The window title, the head and the body all repeat the thought's first
  line (three times; UX-CANON A.7).

OS principle broken: an app has ONE window that reopens where it was; a
settings pane is a pane, not a new window; a dialog never multiplies.

What works (keep): title-bar drag, double-click to zoom and back, ⌃W
closes the front window, ⌘` cycles, the Overview (shot 059), and windows
return at the same place after reload (shot 045). This is the solid part.

Change:
- Runs on is a wing of Settings, not a window. Settings is one window.
- A meeting opens IN the Meetings window (list ▸ detail, Back), not
  stacked over it. The same for a person in People (already so) and a
  project in a Room.
- New windows cascade from the last front window, never over the icon
  column. One toast, in the menu bar, never inside a window.
- Windows size to content: a thought is a sticky; "Nothing needs you" is
  a one-line window.

### R5. A thought is a dead end

Evidence (Job 2): Desk ▸ Write a thought, type, Finish: 4 presses. Then
"turn it into something": Change opens "Find a note… EVERYDAY CONTEXT"
(what is that?), Send offers only "HoldSpeak folder", Finish turns the
button into Resume. No "make a task", no "give to Mira", no "add to Ledger
migration", no "ask about this" (shot 018). ⌘K finds it, but the first,
pre-selected row is `Send … to HoldSpeak folder`: Enter on a search result
writes a file (shot 020). The Object menu (R1) offers nothing. The
meeting, by contrast, turns a transcript into decisions and owed items in
14 s. The thought gets none of that machinery.

OS principle broken: objects are the same kind everywhere; what a meeting
can become, a note can become.

Change: run the meeting's extraction on a finished thought (decisions,
owed items, people named) and show the same OUTCOMES face. Give the thought
window three verbs: Ask about this, Make a task (owner picker from People),
Add to project. Search: the OBJECT row is first; a verb row is never
pre-selected.

### R6. The engine is set, and the desk keeps asking for it

Evidence: the global assignment is the LAN Qwen, READY, and it works
(summary 14 s). Yet: the thought says `NO ENGINE YET`; the Brief says
"not set up: Set up local AI downloads this model"; Speak says "Set up
speech to start · HOLD NONE ANYWHERE"; Runs on lists 2 engines, 5 READY,
2 LIMITED, `WITHOUT AGENTS`, `WITHOUT CALENDAR`, and two "Download" cards
for models he did not ask for (shot 043). Settings' headline is
"Summaries: 192.168.1.43:8080" as if that were the one setting.

OS principle broken: one place for settings, and the system trusts its
own settings.

Change: one line on every face that uses AI: `Qwen3.8 27B · LAN` and
nothing else. "No engine" appears only when the assignment is empty.
Runs on shows the jobs he has (speech, summaries, answers) and the engine
each uses; the catalog of downloads lives behind "Add an engine". Cut the
wiring diagram.

### R7. Demo chrome and seed clutter

Evidence: Change places (8 atmospheres with prose descriptions, shot
058); the Runs on node graph with bezier wires; "Hide the menus"; `SENT
23:55` badge on the Today dock icon; eight seed notes on his desk (Start
here, About me, First dictation, Thread, Current priorities, Weekly
update, 1:1 prep, New thread), which outnumber his own objects 8 to 1 and
reflow when a real object appears (shot 026); the desk gained an
"actions" icon and a "Mira" icon on its own after the meeting; Conductor
opens on "No coder ready · 3 CODERS · Install hooks"; Delivery opens on
"Companion repo · owner/repo · ✕ NO LIVE SOURCE".

OS principle broken: the desktop shows the user's things. A fresh Mac has
an empty desktop and a Dock of apps that open.

Change: seed notes go to a "Start here" folder icon, or are cut; the
desk gets an icon only when he makes an object. Places is a Settings row
(wallpaper), not an app. Conductor and Delivery open on a verb that
works ("Start a coder on Ledger migration") or stay in Go ▸ Tools.

## 3. The cut list

Apps and doors:
- Dock: cut Missed (drawer), Delivery, Terminals, Hide the menus, Places,
  the SENT badge. Keep Today, Speak, Meetings, People, Conductor.
- Windows: cut the Brief chair window (Today ▸ Brief is the brief); cut
  Runs on as a window (a Settings wing); cut Change places as an app;
  cut Calendar snapshot, Context, Components, Commands, Rhythm, Workbenches,
  Activity, Memory, Processes from Go ▸ Tools (keep them reachable by ⌘K
  only until each has a job).
- Meeting: cut the REVIEW wing's second review (or make it read-only);
  cut `MD` `SRT` `Park` from the footer into a "More" row; cut the
  in-window toast.
- Thought: cut "Choose an engine", "Change" (Everyday context), the Send
  block as a permanent panel (Send is a verb that unfolds).

Concepts to merge:
- Needs you = Missed = the bell = the Needs-you icon: one list.
- Brief = Today ▸ Brief.
- Review = Outcomes.
- Thought = Note = Thread: one object, one face, the meeting's outcomes
  machinery.
- Runs on = Settings ▸ Engines. Connections = Settings ▸ Accounts.

Words to remove from every face: PROPOSAL, LINKED, UNREVIEWED, SUPPORTED,
GOVERNING, KEPT, IN INBOX, READS, EVERYDAY CONTEXT, GROUNDING, GROUND THIS
ASK, CTX, SESSION, NO TRAFFIC, USES ASSIGNMENT, ASSIGNMENT, LANDS IN, DRY
RUN, LISTENER, HOLD NONE ANYWHERE, THIS DEVICE (except where something
leaves), RANKED, MTG, DEC, ACT, MEANING, raw JSON, raw ids, engine host
strings on faces that are not Settings.
