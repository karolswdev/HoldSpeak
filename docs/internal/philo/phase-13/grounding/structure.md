## The bar: Job / Workbench 2.0+ / Steroids

The owner's words, 2026-10-01:

> "I do want us to restructure and refine our Desk experiences. We've been soooo heavily investing into the floor, and totally ignoring the desk..."

> "Make sure we want an incredible exeprience. The whoe Workbench 2.0+ on steroids needs to really lean on steroids. Get it?"

- **Job:** J1–J5 below are the unit of value. A surface earns a daily place by helping a Senior Architect with three reports finish one of them. An operator tool can remain available without occupying a daily launcher.
- **Workbench 2.0+:** preserve spatial ownership, consistent gadgets, depth, keyboard verbs and a remembered arrangement. Intuition's documented depth gadget moves a front window behind its peers; its zoom gadget alternates two size/position sets. Our focus/raise and maximize are useful foundations, but not those exact behaviors. [AmigaOS Intuition window documentation](https://wiki.amigaos.net/wiki/Intuition_Windows). Shared screens and screen-depth controls are a separate concept from window depth. [Intuition screen documentation](https://wiki.amigaos.net/wiki/Intuition_Screens).
- **Steroids:** exact-object handoffs, live outcomes at the point of work, fast resume and commands that complete useful steps. These are proposed benefits, not measured improvements. Named screens and automatic arrangement are candidates, not requirements; the owner's arrangement remains his.

Tenets 1–3 constrain the work: no new control plane to compensate for a confusing face. Tenet 4 applies to product copy and user docs; Tenet 5 directs reuse of the existing species; Tenets 6–7 set the ambition and the jobs. A missing optional feature is not a canon violation.
