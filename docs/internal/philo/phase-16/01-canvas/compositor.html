The depth ladder

FRONT · blue, lit, stripes, hard drop + umbra
NEAR · steel, flat bar, veil 6 %, half umbra
FAR · steel in shade, veil 14 %, drop only
SEATED · a dock seat with the lamp

One light, top-left. Every window is drawn from its plane alone. Words on a back window stay readable: it is a veil, never opacity.

The four moments · one easing

Raise 180 ms · lifts 1.2 %, umbra grows, blue; the old front settles back
Open 240 ms · flies out of its object; the frame draws first
Seat / Close 200 ms · shrinks into its dock seat / its object
Arrange 220 ms · tile, stage, exposé, gather, cascade glide; never teleport
Idle never moves. Reduced motion: instant.

The grammar

⌘` raise next · ⌘⇧` send back · ⌘M seat · ⌘W close · ⌘⇧Z zoom
⌘⌥←→ tile · ⌘⏎ stage · ⌘⇧E exposé · ⌘G gather room · Esc back
Seated windows live in the dock. Nowhere else.
Open beside the object. Never cover a bar whole. The band is sacred.

How an internal app is composed

Show the thing, not a form about it. Runs on shows the wiring; the picture IS the state.
Direct manipulation. Drag, press, speak. It applies now, with a receipt in the foot and Undo. No Save, no "Use these".
Live. Every object has a lamp; latency on the wire; a download fills its bar in place.
Try it, everywhere. Every wired thing can be exercised on the spot, with the real answer and its time.
Found before asked. The app discovers (ports, LAN, keys, files) and offers; you accept with one press.
Five state words. READY · LIMITED · BROKEN · WAITING · OFF. Never eleven.
The kit, plus one species of its own, added to the library (here: Switchboard).
