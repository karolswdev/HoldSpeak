The Engines window: the Line's two models, not a Line row

The design (§7) puts two catalog rows in Engines, through the concierge's download path: Phone speech in (Parakeet, 0.6 GB) and Phone voice (Pocket, 0.2 GB), each with its CC-BY-4.0 line in the inspector. They are drawn here with the green new-row tint. The design does not give the Line itself an Engines row, and this canvas does not add one in the recommended direction (A):

  • An Engines row is a thing a job is assigned to (the Used for column, What runs where). Nothing is assigned to the Line: Calls picks it in Adapter, and Calls is its only user.
  • The Line's state already has two homes: Settings ▸ Calls ▸ Line (with the place) and the Calls status bar (one word). A third home is a second teller.
  • The Line's account (a SIP password) is not an engine setting. Direction B below draws the row the brief asked about, so he can see it: "The Line · karol · Ready · 0.4 core per call minute".

What this canvas could not establish

  • Install the Line on karol is a pending verb: the enrolment (node token with calls.line, the build, the systemd user unit) is LINE-3. The canvas assumes karol is already a linked node of the hub (the mesh) so it can be listed before the Line is on it.
  • The Telnyx shape is from the design's §8 and is not verified: the account does not exist. The password custody (HoldSpeak holds the SIP password) needs the owner's word (CONFIG, §14).
  • Test call's target: "the first allowed number or his SIP address" (§10). The canvas draws his SIP app's address as sip:karol@karol-iphone; the real address comes from his SIP app.
  • The per-minute core costs are the spike's measured numbers (§12), for a call minute of 30 s of speech and 20 s of voice.
  • The desk floor is CSS, not a recorded hub shot. No product guard reads docs/ canvases, so the copy here is proven by reading.
