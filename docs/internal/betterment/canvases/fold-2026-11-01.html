A section that opens and closes, without a chevron

Owner, 2026-10-31: "That left chevron something: fuck off. Invent something a lot clearer." Every fold on the desk draws the same ▸ that turns to ▾ (the kit's FoldGadget, gadgets.css summary::before; Disclosure; Today's own folds). This canvas picks one grammar for all of them. The rules for each direction: no triangle, no chevron; the title is a plain word with its count; the state is never shown by colour alone; 44 px at 393; nothing moves the rows around it.

Menus are not folds: a Button that opens a list (More ▾, Project ▾, Later ▾) keeps its small down caret, because it opens a menu over the window, not a section in it. Only the section that opens in place changes.

Recommendation: B, Show · Hide. A verb says what the press does, in his words, at the place his eye ends the line (the right edge, where the row's verbs live on every list). It needs no learning, no icon and no colour: closed says Show, open says Hide. The open section sits under a hairline rule, aligned with its title. At 393 the verb is a 44 px target at the edge, so the whole title row stays readable. A needs him to learn what a filled lamp means; C reads as a link or a tab, and its state is a line length.

Built in the kit: FoldGadget and Disclosure draw the word in place of the triangle, so every fold on the desk changes at once; Today's three folds and the Needs you "Show ▾ / Hide ▴" drop their marks.
