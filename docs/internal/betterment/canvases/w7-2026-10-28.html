(a) Mission control: keep the wallpaper, name every window

What it does today

  • ⌘⇧E, the dock, or Window ▸ Overview puts every shown window into a grid as a live plate: the window itself, scaled by k, never reflowed (web/src/desk/components/window/Expose.tsx).
  • The scrim is one solid fill, --desk-glass #e9edf3 (components/window-chrome.css:118). That is the grey slab: the wallpaper and the floor go away.
  • A plate has no name under it. The pick target carries the name only for a screen reader (sr-only, title), by the PHILO-16 §7 rule “its own title bar, no extra label”. At k ≈ 0.5 the title bar text is 6 px high and does not read.
  • The front window is told only by its own blue title bar (the Room in the shot). Keyboard focus lands on the first plate (the decision), so the one ring on the screen marks the wrong window.
  • The registry already has each window’s name, glyph and kind (windowRegistry.ts, useAllOpenWindows) and the front id (useFrontWindowId). Nothing new is needed from the hub.

A · Captions on a slate veil Recommended

The wallpaper stays, under a slate veil with a 7 px blur: the floor icons blur away (you cannot pick them here), and Jupiter stays. Each plate gets the card step of the depth scale and, 8 px under it, a paper caption: the window’s 20 px glyph, its name in 12 px semibold, and a 3 px rule in the kind’s hue at its foot (decision violet, Project blue, note amber, an app the Workbench blue). The front window has a 3 px vivid-blue ring and a filled blue caption. Keyboard focus starts on the front plate. Hover draws a paper ring; nothing moves.

(b) The Record tile while it records: a breath you can see from across the room

What it does today

  • The breath is a drop-shadow on the orb picture: 1 px at 45 % red to 6 px at 80 % red and back, 2.4 s (components/record-orb.css:36, keyframes desk-next-orb-breath). On the dark dock glass a 6 px red haze around a red orb is a small change in a small area.
  • Under the tile, a 4 px red dot (components/dock.css:478), the size of the “running” dots under the other tiles.
  • The elapsed time shows only on hover, in the label above the tile (RecordOrb.tsx .desk-orb-label; the Meetings tile’s REC 10:42 is hover-only too, Dock.tsx:461). At a glance the tile says nothing about how long.
  • Reduced motion: a still 3 px haze.

A · The ring and the clock Recommended

Around the orb, a 3 px solid red ring that is there the whole time the meeting records: from across the room a red circle on the dark dock says “recording” even at the low point of the breath. Outside it, a red halo opens from 2 px to 8 px and fades from 50 % to 18 %, 2.4 s a breath: box-shadow on a lamp, colour only, the tile never moves. Above the tile, outside the dock plate, a red clock pill: the elapsed time in 12 px semibold white, tabular figures. On hover the label (“Stop recording · 12:04”) takes the pill’s place. The 4 px dot goes: the ring says it.

(c) Context: say what it is for, show one fact, make Save live

What it does today

  • Context is the always-on briefing that every engine run reads first: the saved text goes in as the first system message of every agent, Ask and meeting-intelligence run (holdspeak/constitutional_context.py:1; callers in intel/engine.py, services/ask_service.py, workbench_conductor.py). It is one text for the whole desk, not the context of one thought.
  • The face (web/src/pages/cores/ConstitutionalContextCore.tsx): a toolbar with Save only (Version history when there are revisions), a pad whose placeholder is “Role, stack, rules / One fact per line”, and a status bar “Empty: no run reads it”.
  • Save is already disabled until you type (disabled={!dirty …}); disabled, the primary Button draws grey, so on an empty desk the only verb looks dead, and nothing says what a good fact is.
  • Its purpose is written once, in applications.ts: “The always-on briefing every agent receives.” The window does not show it.

A · The object face Direction

While nothing is saved and the pad is empty, the body is an empty face like the other empty faces (Calls has its telephone): the document object at 64 px, one line, one verb. What this canvas could not establish

  • The 1440 atlas shots are palette-reduced. In desk-recording-1440.png and desk-1440.png the red orb comes out brown (rgb(97,87,79) at its centre): the shots were reduced to stay under 300 KB. The 393 shot keeps the red. The canvas draws the real sprite over the tile. Part of “I cannot see it” may be the shot, not the product; judge the breath live, not on an atlas PNG.
  • Whether every window announces its kind to the registry. announceWindow(…, kind?) takes it as optional; the build lane must map the apps without one to --kind-other.
  • How the overview grid places five or more windows: the room under each row was measured on four plates only.
  • How bright the ring and clock read on your real display from your chair: the “across the room” row is a scale-down, not a distance.
