Programs: the Program language and shaping Direction for PG-LANG

A program has a text: a short, line-based view of the same record the board draws. You read and edit it beside the board. A sentence that you say or type becomes a proposed change to that text, drawn as rows on the board; you press Apply. Design: docs/internal/betterment/programs/PROGRAM-LANGUAGE-2026-10-31.md. Journeys: PROGRAM-JOURNEYS-2026-10-31.md. Every part is a kit piece at real size, in the material of canvases/programs-requirements-2026-10-31.html; the new pieces (DiffRow, ChangeWell, TextPane, QuestionRow, RoundStrip) are drawn from the same tokens.

Recommendation, shaping: S-A, the bar and the well. One sentence field with its mic under the toolbar; the proposed change opens as a well of diff rows right under it, above the board, with Apply and Not this. The rows land on the board when you apply. Your eye never leaves the plan, and the text, the draft and the interview rounds use the same well.

Recommendation, interview rounds: R-A, one question on the board. A strip of round tokens, then one question at a time with up to three offered answers and a field with its mic; your answer turns into diff rows in the same well. Never a form.

The ladder: from two paragraphs to Done

The eight stations. You can enter or leave at each one; hands-off goes 1 → 2 → 4 → 5 → 8 with no press at YOLO.

1 · DroppedA goal: two paragraphs, a note, a sentence on a call.
2 · DraftedThe plan as diff rows. Apply, Ask me first, Not this.
3 · InterviewedRounds, one question at a time. Done with each, or Skip round.
4 · RefinedSteps with briefs, owns, gates, requirements, sized for the engine.
5 · RunningSteps hand, work, check. Shape in one sentence.
6 · VerifiedEach check, review and requirement judged on its commit.
7 · ProvenPRs merged, the end check, the evidence, the dw story.
8 · Done"Done · 18 of 18 met". A dreaming night opens again at 23:30.

What this canvas could not establish

  • The TextPane is drawn in CSS; whether a textarea with an overlaid gutter keeps 60 fps on a 400-line text is not measured (the design starts there, with no editor library).
  • The engine's times ("qwen3.8-27b · 2.8 s") are targets from the design, not measurements; no shaping call has run.
  • The estimates ("18k of 64k") follow the design's rule with a guessed fixed cost for pi; the real cost is measured in LANG8.
  • The desk floor is CSS, not a recorded shot. No product guard reads docs/ canvases, so the words here are proven by reading only.
