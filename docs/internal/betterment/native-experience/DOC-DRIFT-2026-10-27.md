(`WINDOW-PROGRAM-2026-10-27.md`). It compares README, the `docs/*.md`
user guides, the design canon and the native-experience packet with the
live desk on `main` (`ce5ec8325`, 2026-10-27). For: the orchestrator who
merges and parks, and the next docs or screenshot lane. True on 2026-10-27.

keys), `web/src/desk/components/DeskMenuBar.tsx`, `web/src/desk/verbRegistry.ts`,

Status words: **fixed** (edited in this lane), **marked** (history or parked
face: a dated status line added, body kept), **park?** (proposal; the
