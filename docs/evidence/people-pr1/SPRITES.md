Prompt:

> A compact confidential people leadership ledger: a bound dark slate field
> notebook or rolodex with three abstract person tabs and one small signal-orange
> commitment check tab. Workbench desktop icon, crisp silhouette, no text, no
> numbers, no charts, no surveillance symbols, no heart, no cloud, no microphone.
> Palette: charcoal slate, paper white, signal orange. Transparent background.

The three marks indicate relationships, not a person score, ranking, sentiment, or
activity signal. The orange tab indicates explicit follow-through.
